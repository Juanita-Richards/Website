Research & Technical Portfolio

A selection of projects spanning virtual and augmented reality, human-computer interaction, embodied interaction, motion tracking, machine learning, and immersive systems development.

Virtual Reality Augmented Reality Human-Computer Interaction Unity Motion Tracking Machine Learning
01

VR · Embodiment · Learning Transfer

Assembly Skill Transfer in Virtual Reality

Investigating how different VR interaction methods influence the transfer of assembly skills from virtual environments to real-world tasks. The study compares hand tracking, controllers, and passive observation while examining performance and embodiment.

FocusEmbodied interaction, learning transfer, memory
MethodsHuman-subject studies, VR experiments, behavioral analysis
ToolsUnity, Meta Quest, C#, motion tracking
02

XR · Data Collection · Unity

Multimodal Motion Tracking Pipeline

Developed a Unity-based pipeline for capturing body and hand movement during immersive interaction studies. The system records position, quaternion rotation, Euler rotation, and finger-joint data for later behavioral and machine-learning analysis.

DataBody pose, hand joints, position, rotation
OutputStructured CSV datasets for analysis
ToolsUnity, C#, Meta SDK
03

AR · Spatial Perception · User Studies

Perception & Proxemics in Augmented Reality

Research examining how people perceive and respond to virtual humans and virtual entities in augmented-reality environments, including spatial relationships and perceptual effects across real and virtual space.

FocusSpatial perception, proxemics, virtual humans
MethodsControlled experiments, quantitative analysis
AreaAugmented reality and human perception
04

VR · Avatars · Culture

Cultural Identity & Avatar Embodiment

Studied how avatar-user matching can influence a person's sense of embodiment in virtual reality, with emphasis on cultural and identity-related characteristics represented through avatars.

FocusAvatars, embodiment, representation
MethodsVR user studies, questionnaires, statistical analysis
AreaHuman-centered immersive computing
05

ML · Human Behavior · Multimodal Data

Human Behavior Prediction from Motion Data

Exploring machine-learning approaches that use motion and behavioral data collected during interactive experiences to identify patterns and predict interpersonal or participant-level characteristics.

FocusBehavior modeling, prediction, human motion
DataMultimodal and spatial datasets
AreaMachine learning for HCI
06

Unity · Interaction Design · Experiment Systems

Immersive Experiment Tooling

Built reusable Unity systems for timed tasks, participant questionnaires, interaction lockouts, automatic completion handling, object tracking, and experimental data collection to support reliable XR research workflows.

FeaturesTimers, questionnaires, event handling, logging
GoalReliable and repeatable experimental workflows
ToolsUnity, C#, Meta XR
